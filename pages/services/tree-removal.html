When a tree needs to come down

Most removals we do around Pensacola fall into a few groups:

  • Dead or dying trees. Pines hit by beetles, oaks rotting at the base, anything with large dead limbs. Dead wood gets brittle fast in our heat and humidity.
  • Trees leaning toward something. A house, a fence, a driveway or power lines. A new lean after a storm is worth a look right away.
  • Trees that have outgrown the spot. Roots lifting a slab, branches rubbing the roof, or a tree that blocks the view or the sun for a garden.
  • Clearing for a project. A pool, an addition, a shed or a new fence line.

How we take a tree down

Very few yards in Pensacola have room to drop a whole tree at once, so we usually climb it and take it down in sections, lowering limbs and trunk pieces with ropes so nothing lands on the house, the fence or the flower beds. As one customer put it, Dave “dropped the branches and tree right where he said he would.”

Once the tree is down, we cut up the wood, haul off the branches and debris, and rake up so the yard is clean when we leave.

Questions about tree removal

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Pensacola?

Some trees do, depending on where you live and the type and size of the tree. Rules differ between the City of Pensacola, Escambia County and Santa Rosa County. Ask us when you send your photos and we'll tell you what we know, but check with your city or county before work starts.

Will you remove the stump too?

Yes, if you want it gone. We can grind the stump below ground level in the same visit. Just mention it when you ask for your quote.

Can you take down a tree near my house or power lines?

Trees near houses and fences are routine: we take them down in sections and lower the pieces. If a tree is touching or tangled in power lines, the utility may need to make it safe first. Send a photo and we'll tell you.
