What we handle after a storm

  • Trees down across yards, driveways and fences
  • Limbs hanging in the canopy that could still fall
  • Split, cracked or newly leaning trees that need to come down
  • Branches and debris piled across the yard
  • Stumps left behind by uprooted trees

Stay safe first

  • Stay away from any tree or limb touching a power line, and call FPL or your electric co-op.
  • Don't stand under hanging or cracked limbs. They can fall without warning.
  • If a tree is on your house, take photos for your insurance company before anything is moved.

How to get on the schedule

Text photos of the damage and your address to (850) 426-4220. Photos let us see what equipment the job needs and give you a price without a site visit, which matters when everyone's phone is ringing after a storm.

Want to avoid the storm damage in the first place? Trimming out dead and weak limbs before hurricane season is the best prevention.

Questions about storm cleanup

Do you work with insurance claims?

We can give you a written quote and photos of the job for your insurance company. Coverage is between you and your insurer, so check your policy.

Can you remove a tree that fell on my house?

Send photos first. If it's on the roof, we'll tell you what it takes to get it off safely. Don't try to cut it yourself.

How soon can you come after a storm?

It depends on how many calls come in. Texting photos is the fastest way to get a quote and a spot on the schedule.
