Meet Dave Lau, the man behind Log Hog

22 years in the U.S. Navy, now taking care of trees across Pensacola, one yard at a time.

Dave Lau was born and raised in Mobile, Alabama, and served 22 years in the U.S. Navy, retiring as a Navy Chief.

With a background in aviation mechanics, he brings precision, safety and hard work to every tree job. For Dave, tree care is more than a job. It's a passion: he loves making trees look their best and seeing customers happy with their yards.

A devoted Christian, Dave aims to serve every customer with integrity and respect. When you hire Log Hog, you get a skilled, reliable partner who cares about the beauty and safety of your property.
