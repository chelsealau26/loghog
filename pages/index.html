Questions folks ask us

How do I get a quote?

Text a photo of the tree or job and your address to (850) 426-4220, or give us a call. Quotes are free and there's no obligation.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Log Hog Tree Service is licensed and insured.

Do you clean up after the job?

Always. We haul away the branches and debris and rake up, so your yard is clean when we leave.

Can you grind the stump when you remove a tree?

Yes. Stump grinding can be added to any tree removal. Just mention it when you ask for your quote.

What are your hours?

Monday through Saturday, 8am to 5pm.

What areas do you serve?

Pensacola and our neighbors nearby, including Gulf Breeze, Pensacola Beach, Perdido Key, Cantonment, Pace, Milton, and Lillian and Seminole, AL. See the full list on our service areas page.
