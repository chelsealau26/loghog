What to send us

The more we can see, the more accurate your quote. Here's the shot list:

  1. A photo of the whole tree, from far enough back to see the top
  2. A photo of the base or stump
  3. Anything close by: the house, fence, pool, driveway or power lines
  4. Your address
  5. What you'd like done: removed, trimmed, stump ground or hauled
  6. Anything we should know, like a locked gate and how wide it is
