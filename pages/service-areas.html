Trees we know well

Live oaks, laurel and water oaks, longleaf and slash pines, magnolias, crepe myrtles and every kind of palm. Sandy soil, a long hurricane season and our summer storms make dead limbs and leaning pines common around here, so regular trimming and quick cleanup after storms matter more on the Gulf Coast than most places.
