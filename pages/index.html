Meet Log Hog

We're a small, local crew, and we take every job personally. Log Hog is owned by Dave Lau, who grew up in Mobile and served 22 years in the U.S. Navy, retiring as a Chief. He spent that career working on aircraft, where you do the job carefully and get it right the first time. That's how our whole crew works.

We show up when we say we will, treat your home like it belongs to family, and leave the yard cleaner than we found it. If there's a tree that's been worrying you, send us a picture. You'll get a straight answer and a fair price.
