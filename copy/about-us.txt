Meet the Man Who Will Make Your Tree Dreams Come True
Dave Lau, founder of Log Hog, was born and raised in Mobile, AL, and served 22 years in the U.S. Navy, retiring as a Navy Chief. With a background in aviation mechanics, he brings precision, safety, and hard work to every tree service job.
For Dave, tree care is more than a job—it’s a passion. Licensed and insured, he loves making trees look their best and seeing customers pleased with their outdoor spaces. A devoted Christian, Dave strives to serve others with integrity and respect, guided by his strong faith.
