Palm trimming

We cut off dead and dying fronds, seed pods and flower stalks, which keeps palms looking clean and stops heavy fronds from dropping on cars, roofs and people. We leave the healthy green fronds alone: over-trimming (the “hurricane cut”) weakens palms.

Palm skinning

Skinning removes the old frond bases (the “boots”) to give the trunk a smooth, clean look. It's popular on sabal and Canary Island date palms around Pensacola and the beaches.

Bushes and shrubs

We shape overgrown hedges, bushes and shrubs and cut back anything crowding the house, walkways or windows.

Questions about palm trimming & skinning

How often should palms be trimmed?

Most palms look best with a trim once or twice a year. Trimming out seed pods before they drop saves a lot of mess.

Is it bad to cut off green fronds?

Yes. Palms feed through their green fronds, so we leave them on. We only remove dead, dying and hanging fronds unless they're in the way.

Do you trim tall palms?

Yes. Send a photo and let us know roughly how tall it is.
